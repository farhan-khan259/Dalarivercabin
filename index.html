About Bagarstugan

A cozy cottage with stories of the old wood-fired oven, located between two mountains beside the river.

Bagarstugan (The Baker's Cottage) is nestled between two mountains by a serene river. Formerly the farm’s baking place until 1970, it once held a large wood-fired oven for making flatbread. Today the cottage has been renovated with modern comfort while preserving its cozy and warm atmosphere.

The ground floor offers a welcoming living room and kitchen with natural materials and soft light. Upstairs there are two peaceful bedrooms where the quiet woodlands and river air set the tone for restful nights.

Along the lake you can fish for pike, perch and salmon. A wood-fired sauna can be booked through the host. The hostess lives on the farm and enjoys helping guests plan local experiences.
